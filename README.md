# SafeHaven AI – TechSprint Hackathon

SafeHaven AI is an AI-powered personal safety solution designed to proactively detect indoor and outdoor risks and assist users during emergencies using Google AI technologies.

---

## Problem Statement
People often lack real-time awareness of safety risks both inside homes (fire, gas leaks, medical emergencies) and outside (unsafe routes, isolated areas). Existing solutions are reactive and fragmented, providing limited preventive intelligence.

---

## Proposed Solution
SafeHaven AI acts as a smart safety companion that analyzes environmental and location-based data to detect risks early, suggest safer alternatives, and trigger emergency assistance when needed.

---

## Key Features
- AI-based indoor hazard detection
- Outdoor safety and unsafe route identification
- One-tap Emergency SOS with live location sharing
- Real-time alerts and safety recommendations
- Centralized safety dashboard

---

## Google Technologies Used
- **Google Gemini** – AI-based risk analysis and safety recommendations
- **Google Firebase** – Real-time notifications and emergency data handling
- **Google Maps API** – Live location tracking and safe route mapping
- **Google Cloud Platform** – Scalable backend support

---

## MVP Status
This repository contains the MVP-level structure, UI placeholders, and documented AI workflow for SafeHaven AI.  
The focus is on demonstrating feasibility, architecture, and AI integration rather than a full production build.

---

## Future Enhancements
- IoT sensor integration for real-time indoor monitoring
- Predictive risk analysis using historical data
- Wearable device support for SOS activation
- Multilingual support and local emergency integration
